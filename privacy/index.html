Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Sudoku is a puzzle game for Android published by Play, Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: nothing. Sudoku collects no data at all.

What Sudoku collects

Nothing. Sudoku has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no leaderboards. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or device information, and it does not know who you are. Because no data is collected, none is shared with any third party.

What stays on your phone

Sudoku saves a few things so you can pick up where you left off:

  • the puzzle in progress, with your digits, crossed candidates and timer
  • your stats for each level (puzzles started and completed, best and average times, best scores)
  • which lessons you have finished
  • your settings (sound, Show candidates, Auto Cross, Light or Dark skin, the level you last played and whether you have answered the offer of the first lesson)

These are kept only in the app's own private storage on your phone. The game never sends them anywhere and we cannot see them. If you move to a new phone with Android's own phone transfer, Android may copy them across for you. You can reset your stats at any time in the game's Settings. Uninstalling Sudoku deletes everything it saved, and you can also clear it in your phone's settings, under Apps, Sudoku, Storage, Clear data.

Internet and permissions

Sudoku works fully offline. The game never connects to the internet, and it asks for no Android permissions. Puzzles are made on your phone by the game itself, and Expert puzzles come from a set built into the app.

The Buy me a coffee button

Sudoku has an optional Buy me a coffee button on its title screen, menus, Settings, About and finish screens, and a coffee cup under the Pause button while you play. Tapping it opens a web page at buymeacoffee.com in your phone's web browser. The game itself sends nothing when you tap it. If you visit that page or choose to support us there, Buy Me a Coffee's own privacy policy applies to that visit, not this one.

Google Play

If you install Sudoku from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and your device under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive any personal information from this.

Children

Sudoku is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Sudoku ever starts collecting any data, this policy will say so before that version of the game is released.
